Northward Building

Downtown Fairbanks, Alaska

The largest building in the portfolio and the busiest. Apartments above, commercial tenants at street level, in the middle of downtown Fairbanks. Industrial

Frontier Industrial

Prudhoe Bay, Alaska

Eight warehouses on the North Slope. Shop space, heated storage and laydown for operators working the Prudhoe Bay field. Materials come up the Dalton Highway by truck, and building new on the Slope still costs a multiple of what it costs in Fairbanks, which is most of the reason existing slope industrial holds its value.
