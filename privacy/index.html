Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026. Applies to www.fairbankscp.com.

Fairbanks Commercial Properties operates this website. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. If anything here is unclear, write to maiia@fairbankscp.com.

Information we collect

We collect what you give us. If you fill in a form on this site, that is your name, your email address, your phone number if you provide one, and whatever you write in the message. If you call or email us, we keep that correspondence so we can deal with your inquiry.

Our web host records standard server information such as the pages requested and the time of the request. We do not run advertising trackers on this site and we do not sell data to anyone.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and arrange viewings or meetings.
  • To administer tenancies, leases and maintenance where you are a tenant or applicant.
  • To keep records we are required by law to keep.

How we disclose information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only where it is necessary: with service providers who process it on our behalf under contract, with professional advisers, and where the law or a court requires it.

Mobile information and SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to text messages from us, message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to end messages at any time, or HELP for help.

How long we keep it

Inquiries are kept for as long as we may reasonably need them to follow up, and tenancy records for as long as the law and our insurers require after a tenancy ends.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Write to maiia@fairbankscp.com or to the office address below.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
